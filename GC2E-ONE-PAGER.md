# GLOBAL CONNECTIONS TO EDUCATION AND EMPLOYMENT (GC2E)
## A free, adoptable framework for shelters, libraries, rehabs, and community groups

### THE PROBLEM
People leaving homelessness, addiction treatment, or incarceration have no stable identity on paper. No email, no records, no way for an employer or school to reach them. The system assumes a digital presence that doesn't exist.

### THE FIX: ONE INTAKE, THREE HANDOFFS

#### 1. FREE GMAIL — THE GLOBAL ADDRESS
**Why it matters:**
- It's the closest thing to a universal ID in America. Employers, schools, government portals, and housing applications all expect one.
- It's free forever, works on any phone or library computer, and doesn't expire.
- It gives the person a place to receive job offers, class confirmations, benefits notices, and family contact — one inbox that follows them anywhere.

**Why not just a phone number:**
- Numbers change. SIMs get lost, stolen, or disconnected when funds run out.
- An email address is a stable anchor. It can be accessed from any device, anywhere, at any time.
- It creates a paper trail: proof of application dates, acceptance letters, appointment history.

**Google's trade-off (be honest with them):**
- Google reads metadata to serve ads. That's the price of free.
- Mitigation: use it only for job/school/official business. Don't store sensitive documents or personal photos there. Keep medical and legal records in a separate, encrypted location or on paper in a folder.

**How to set it up (10 minutes, no money):**
- Go to gmail.com on any browser — library, shelter computer, or a borrowed phone.
- Create the account using a name the person will use professionally (first + last, or first initial + last).
- Write the address on a card and keep it in their wallet alongside ID.

#### 2. THE RELEASE OF INFORMATION
- One form, signed by the person, naming who may be told: "I'm here, I'm okay, here's how to reach me."
- Mirrors hospital designated-contact lists. Simple language, one signature, dated.
- Without it, agencies can't even confirm someone is safe — which is how families lose people for years.

#### 3. THE JOB-AND-SCHOOL HANDOFF
- Before discharge or exit, connect the person to an employment or education partner (like Global Connections to Employment at Lakeview).
- The goal: a scheduled interview, a class enrollment, or an application submitted BEFORE they walk out the door.
- A plan started inside beats a plan started on the street.

### WHO CAN ADOPT THIS
- Homeless shelters
- Addiction treatment centers
- Libraries (already the front line for free computer access)
- Faith communities
- Reentry programs
- Any group that sees the same people cycle through without a digital anchor

### THE ONE RULE
Never overwrite history. Every intake, every release, every handoff gets dated and filed. The archive is the safety net when memory fails.
